What you get

One plain-text email on any day a new permit lands: date, address, teardown or electrical, the firm doing the work and its mailing address. Nothing on a quiet day.

A permit is permission, not a piece of equipment. Nothing in the record says what is inside. The firm on the row is who to call, and we give you the number of the permit so you can check it yourself.
